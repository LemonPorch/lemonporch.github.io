Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy covers the Lemon Porch website and Lemon Porch Publisher, the tool we use to send our own videos to our own social media accounts.

Website

This website has no sign-in, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. It is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in its own privacy statement.

Lemon Porch Publisher

Lemon Porch Publisher is used only by the Lemon Porch team with the Lemon Porch accounts. When we connect a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok gives the tool:

  • basic profile information (user.info.basic: the account's open ID, display name and avatar), used only to confirm the right account is connected;
  • permission to upload a video as a draft to that account's TikTok inbox (video.upload).

The access and refresh tokens are stored only in the operating system's encrypted keychain on our own computer. They are never sent to anyone other than TikTok. We keep a local record of which of our videos were sent and their upload status. We do not collect, sell or share any data about other TikTok users or viewers, and the tool does not read messages, comments, followers or analytics.

The same applies to Instagram: the tool stores an access token for our own Instagram professional account and uses it only to upload and publish our own Reels.

Deleting data

Removing the tool's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions) or in Instagram stops all access at once. We then delete the stored tokens from our keychain.

Children

The tool is not meant for, and does not collect data from, anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything above changes. Questions: contact us through the Lemon Porch YouTube channel.
