Current series: Lantern Inn

A cozy inn-keeping game made in Phaser. Guests arrive at night, you cook and serve before their patience runs out, and a few regulars come back with stories that change depending on how well you remember them.

Episode 1: a first build that scored 47 and its fix to 60. Episode 2: shuffled days, things to spend coins on and two regulars, aiming for 70.

Lemon Porch Publisher

Lemon Porch Publisher is our own publishing tool. When a short clip is finished, it sends the video to the Lemon Porch account on TikTok as a draft in the TikTok inbox, and schedules the same clip as an Instagram Reel. The draft is not public: we open it in the TikTok app, check it, write the caption and post it ourselves.

It signs in with TikTok Login Kit and uses only the permissions to read basic profile info and to upload a video as a draft. It never posts directly, reads messages or followers, or collects anything from viewers. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
